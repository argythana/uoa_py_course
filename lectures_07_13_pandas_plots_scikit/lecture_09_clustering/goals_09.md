# Lecture 09: Clustering

## Learning Goals

- Understand unsupervised learning and the concept of clustering (grouping unlabelled observations).
- Implement KMeans clustering using scikit-learn on the mall customers dataset.
- Evaluate clusters using WCSS (inertia) and Silhouette Score; choose the number of clusters K.
- Understand centroid initialization effects and bad initialization pitfalls (`n_init`, `init`).
- Awareness of other clustering algorithms available in scikit-learn.

## Files

- `lec_09a_kmeans_clustering.ipynb` — KMeans tutorial: theory, mall customers dataset, WCSS, silhouette score.
- `lec_09a_kmeans_clustering_with_initialization.ipynb` — Extended version including centroid initialization.
- `lec_09b_kmeans_centroids_initialization.ipynb` — Centroid initialization effects on iris data.
- `lec_09c_other_clustering_algos.ipynb` — Links to alternative clustering algorithms.
