# Lecture 15: PyTorch Step by Step

## Learning Goals

- Understand PyTorch Datasets and DataLoaders for loading and batching data.
- Load pre-built datasets (FashionMNIST, CIFAR10) using torchvision.
- Define a Convolutional Neural Network architecture in PyTorch.
- Train a classifier: define loss function, optimizer, training loop.
- Test network performance on held-out test data.

- `lec_15a_pytorch_data_tutorial.ipynb` — PyTorch Datasets & DataLoaders: loading FashionMNIST, batching.
- `lec_15b_pytorch_cifar10_tutorial.ipynb` — Training an image classifier on CIFAR10: CNN, loss, training loop.
